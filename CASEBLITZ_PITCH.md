### The Results (30 seconds)

- Average fasting glucose: 135 mg/dL

**After (Intervention):**

**Improvement: 11%**

**Evidence Strength: Strong** (100% adherence, 5/5 measurements)

## 🎤 Tough Questions - Prepared Answers

### Q: How is this different from existing diabetes apps?

**A**: Existing apps provide coaching and generic advice. We discover patterns specific to each patient through data analysis, then validate them through structured experiments. The system learns what works for YOU, not what works for average patients.

---

### Q: What if the ML makes a wrong recommendation?

**A**: Our ML doesn't make medical decisions. It only detects correlations in data. All interventions come from a clinician-approved library (e.g., "eat dinner earlier", "walk after meals"). Patients test them voluntarily. Doctors have oversight. Safety is paramount.

---

### Q: Where's the clinical evidence this works?

**A**: Digital diabetes interventions show 0.3-0.5% HbA1c reduction in multiple RCTs. Personalized N-of-1 trials show better adherence than generic advice. We're building the platform to generate our own evidence through pilots. We're not claiming clinical outcomes yet—we're claiming a better way to discover what works for each patient.

---

### Q: Why would hospitals pay for this?

**A**: Diabetes complications cost hospitals ₹50,000-5,00,000 per patient. Better adherence reduces complications by 20-30%. If we charge ₹300/month (₹3,600/year), hospitals get massive ROI. Plus, better outcomes = better ratings = more patients.

---

### Q: How will you scale to millions of users?

**A**: Software scales. Once validated in pilots, we expand regionally through hospital partnerships and insurer partnerships. Government NPCDCS program reaches millions—we can integrate. Target: 10,000 users in 12 months, 100,000 in 24 months.

---

### Q: What about data privacy and security?

**A**: JWT authentication, role-based access, patient-controlled consent, audit logging. DPDP Act compliance planned. Data stored in India (AWS Mumbai). No third-party data sharing. Patient owns their data.

---

### Q: Who are your competitors?

**A**: BeatO and Sugar.fit (India), Livongo (US), Virta (US). But we're differentiated by personalized experiments vs generic coaching. Our moat is the behavioral data and learning engine that improves over time.

---

### Q: What if patients don't engage?

**A**: Traditional apps have 5-10% DAU/MAU. We expect better because insights are personal and actionable. But we'll track engagement closely. If a patient disengages, we alert their doctor. Gamification and family support features coming.

---

### Q: Can this work for other diseases?

**A**: Yes! The experiment framework works for hypertension, PCOS, chronic kidney disease, etc. Diabetes is the beachhead. Once validated, we expand to other chronic diseases. Multi-disease platform = 10x bigger market.
